Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

ResQNet is a volunteer-run community tool designed to coordinate emergency response efforts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

2. Data Collection

To effectively coordinate help, we collect the following data when you submit a report or register as a volunteer:

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates and resolved addresses to map incidents.
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers and names to allow coordinators and volunteers to reach you.
  • Incident Details: Descriptions, categories, and voice transcripts you provide regarding the emergency.

3. Data Usage

We collect this data solely for the purpose of coordinating emergency response. Your information is used by our AI triage system to assess severity, and by verified human coordinators to dispatch community volunteers.

4. Data Retention

We retain incident data as long as necessary for community safety and reporting purposes. Our systems undergo regular cleanups to remove stale or resolved incidents.

5. Data Visibility

Your detailed incident reports (including phone numbers and exact locations) are visible only to authenticated, verified coordinators. General anonymized data may be visible on public heatmap metrics, but personal identifiers are restricted.

6. Not an Official Service

ResQNet is not an official government or emergency service. We cannot guarantee response times or data privacy equivalent to official emergency dispatch centers.
